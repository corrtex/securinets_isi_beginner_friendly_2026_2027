The Keeper held the one deck no challenger ever beat. When he died, his vault was robbed, but the thieves left two cards on the floor, worthless alone: Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity. They never knew what they held.

You did. The Keeper had torn the five pieces of Exodia out of play and hidden them where no single hand could draw them whole, across these two cards.

One journal entry survived the fire:

“Drew the last piece tonight. I still scream it at an empty room, ‘exodia_obliterate!!!’ The Forbidden One is whole again.”

Draw the cards, recover all five pieces, and assemble the Forbidden One.
